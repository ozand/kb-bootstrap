# Positioning, strengths and weaknesses

Read when the brief asks how products present themselves and what users think.

## Two kinds of evidence — keep them apart

1. **What the vendor says** — tagline, target audience, differentiators on the home page,
   pricing tiers. Capture the home page once for this.
2. **What users say** — public review pages (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot), Reddit threads,
   forums, GitHub issues, app-store reviews. Capture only publicly visible parts.


## Rules

- Quote or closely paraphrase; give the count of independent sources that agree
  ("3 of 5 captured reviews"). No invented percentages — a number must appear in raw.
- One angry review is an anecdote; a repeated complaint across sources is a finding.
- Comparison articles written by a competitor ("X vs Y" on Y's site) are biased — label them.
- End with **our position**: where the user's product is stronger, where it is weaker, and
  which competitor weakness is an opening. Keep it grounded in the matrices above.
